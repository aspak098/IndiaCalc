FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. The basic calculators on this demo are free to use.

Is the EMI result exact?

The result uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula. Actual lender charges, taxes, fees or rounding can change the final repayment.

Can I add more calculators?

Yes. The JavaScript is modular, so additional calculators can be added as new cards and functions.
